Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Hawkeye (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the Hawkeye website, Hawkeye Community (open-source software), and Hawkeye Lab (subscription software).

Contact: hawkeye18485@gmail.com

Website (hawkeye-leo.github.io/hawkeye)

This site is static documentation hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics on these pages. GitHub may process standard server logs as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message content, and any attachments you send.

Hawkeye Community

Hawkeye Community runs locally on your Windows machine. We do not receive telemetry from the application by default. The !support command generates a local system report; that report is sent to us only if you choose to email it.

Hawkeye Lab — subscriptions and licensing

Hawkeye Lab subscriptions and payments are processed by Creem, our Merchant of Record. Creem collects payment and billing information needed to complete your purchase. See Creem’s privacy policy for how they handle payment data.

When you activate Hawkeye Lab, the application sends your license key and a machine identifier to our license service so Creem can bind the license to your device. We do not use this for advertising.

How we use information

  • Provide support and respond to requests
  • Operate license activation, validation, and deactivation
  • Improve the product and fix issues
  • Meet legal and compliance obligations

Retention and security

We keep support email and license-related records only as long as needed for support, billing, and license management. License API keys and signing material are stored on server-side infrastructure, not in client applications.

Your choices

You may request access to or deletion of support correspondence by emailing hawkeye18485@gmail.com. Subscription billing changes and cancellations for Hawkeye Lab are handled through Creem’s customer portal and checkout flows.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

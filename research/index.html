We publish research write-ups related to game security from Hawkeye live host triage on authorized systems. We validate our findings on Hawkeye Community and Hawkeye Lab; IDA and Cheat Engine where the mechanism warrants it.

Authorized research only. Do not use against third-party software, games, or systems you do not administer.

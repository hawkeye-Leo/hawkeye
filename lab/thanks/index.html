Quick start

  1. Download and extract the ZIP from your Creem email.
  2. Run Hawkeye.exe as Administrator.
  3. Complete Lab Environment until the status bar reads Driver OK.
  4. Open the License dialog and enter your key to activate on this machine.
  5. Try !analyze -load -run -demo for a guided first run, or open the Lab guide below.
